Cricket Overview

Cricket is a bat-and-ball sport that originated in England and is widely played in countries like India, Australia, and Pakistan. The game involves two teams, each taking turns to bat and bowl. The batting team aims to score as many runs as possible, while the bowling team tries to dismiss the batsmen by getting them out. Cricket matches can vary in length: One Day Internationals (ODIs) are limited to 50 overs per team, T20 matches have 20 overs per team, and Test matches can last up to five days. Runs are scored by hitting the ball and running between the wickets, or by hitting boundaries (4 or 6 runs) depending on how the ball is struck.
