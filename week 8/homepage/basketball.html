Basketball Overview

Basketball is a fast-paced sport played between two teams of five players each. The objective is to score by shooting the ball through the opposing team's hoop, a net-like structure mounted 10 feet above the ground. Points are awarded for baskets made from different areas on the court, with shots taken from beyond the three-point line earning three points, and other field goals worth two. The game is divided into two halves or four quarters, depending on the league or level of play. It requires skills like dribbling, shooting, passing, and defense. Players must also be quick on their feet and work well as a team to outsmart their opponents.
