Football Overview

Football, also known as soccer in some countries, is the world’s most popular sport, played between two teams of eleven players each. The aim is to score by getting the ball into the opposing team’s goal, which is defended by a goalkeeper. Players use their feet to control, pass, and shoot the ball, although goalkeepers can use their hands within the penalty area. A standard match is played over two halves of 45 minutes each, with a short halftime break. Football requires teamwork, coordination, and strategy, with players specializing in different positions such as defenders, midfielders, and forwards. The sport has a rich history, with the modern version being codified in the 19th century in England. It is governed globally by FIFA, and major tournaments like the FIFA World Cup attract billions of viewers worldwide.
